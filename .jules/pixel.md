## 2025-04-02 - Added Confirmation for Assignment Deletion and Improved Form Accessibility
**Problem:** Destructive delete actions lacked confirmation dialogues, creating a high risk of accidental data loss. Input fields in forms lacked proper `htmlFor` bindings to their underlying inputs, causing screen readers and test libraries to fail associating labels with inputs.
**Context:** The app's `ClassroomDetail` page handles assignment deletion for teachers. If accidentally clicked, it could lead to irrecoverable deletion of class assignments and related data. The `InputField` is widely used across the authentication module, so fixing its accessibility has high impact across login and sign-up flows.
**Solution:** Added `window.confirm` to `ClassroomDetail.jsx` delete button to ensure intentionality. Added `htmlFor={name}` to the `<label>` in `InputField.jsx` directly associating it with the `<input>` element `id`, fixing `getByLabelText` errors and improving screen reader accessibility. Also added `title` and `aria-label` to icon-only interactive elements.
