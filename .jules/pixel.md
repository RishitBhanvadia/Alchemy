## 2024-05-18 - Improve Empty State Consistency
**Problem:** The `EmptyState` component was somewhat generic, missing a coherent connection to the glassmorphism and neon aesthetic of the application, and the history table specifically could look completely blank while loading.
**Context:** Alchemistry uses a distinct "neon on dark glass" style (`.glass-panel`, `.neon-glow`). When an empty state is shown (like on the History page or Student Dashboard), it should feel like an integrated part of the futuristic lab environment, not a generic fallback.
**Solution:** Transformed the `EmptyState` component into a polished, glassmorphism-styled card (`.glass-panel` inspired) with a dashed semi-transparent border and a backdrop blur. Added a gentle, continuous floating animation to the icon, drop shadows, and neon glow effects for the text and action button. The action button also received a `type="button"` attribute to satisfy accessibility and explicitly define its role. The new aesthetic perfectly matches the dark, futuristic tone of the rest of the application.
