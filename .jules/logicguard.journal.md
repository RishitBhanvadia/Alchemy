## 2025-03-02 - Incorrect rounding errors during chemistry combination logic and division by zero vulnerability
**Bug:** The logic failed when all parameters passed were zero, resulting in a division by zero (`add === 0`). Additionally, the logic for fixing the sum of combined inputs when it exceeded or fell below exactly 100 correctly iterated its checks using `if (final_add > 100)` and `if (final_add < 100)`, meaning only a single `-10` or `+10` adjustment was performed regardless of how far from 100 the sum was.
**Root Cause:** Dividing by zero due to an initial lack of defensive programming, and attempting iterative operations via simple `if` structures rather than actual `while` loops that continuously check loop boundaries.
**Learning:** For multi-step input normalization that relies on discrete rounding intervals (e.g. increments of 10), it is important to always use a `while` loop that recalculates bounds if operations might require repeated iterations rather than solitary `if` operations that only work for minor rounding differences.
