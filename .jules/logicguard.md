## 2025-03-05 - Fix Chemistry Mixing Calculation Normalisation & Rounding Bugs
**Bug:** The chemical simulation mixed concentration API was normalising initial inputs only when their sum was `< 100`, ignoring when it was `> 100`. Furthermore, rounding corrections for sum inaccuracies (due to nearest-10 conversions) were done via a single `if` statement per case (either `+10` or `-10`), leading to incorrect final combinations that failed to strictly add up to 100 in many common mixing scenarios.
**Root Cause:** The logic assumed single `+10` or `-10` additions were sufficient to adjust rounding errors back to 100, which is mathematically false when large combinations of multi-value percentages are rounded simultaneously. Normalisation was also bounded incorrectly to `sum < 100`, leaving sums like 400 unresolved.
**Learning:** For continuous variable adjustments designed to satisfy a fixed sum constraint (e.g., percentages summing exactly to 100), standard normalisation (`value / total * 100`) must be applied globally for any sum `!== 100` and `> 0`, and rounding adjustments must be iterative (i.e. `while` loops) rather than single-pass conditionals to account for collective rounding shifts.
