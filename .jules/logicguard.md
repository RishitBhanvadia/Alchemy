## 2024-05-24 - [Fix chemical mixture normalization logic]
**Bug:** When normalizing chemical concentrations that sum to < 100, the server logic caused a division by zero error if all concentrations were 0 (returning NaN), and the subsequent rounding adjustments only ran once, failing to reach a 100% total due to rounding differences.
**Root Cause:** The normalization `if (add < 100)` check didn't protect against `add === 0`, causing `NaN`. The rounding adjustments for `final_add < 100` and `final_add > 100` used `if` statements instead of `while` loops, meaning the adjustment could fall short if rounding off by > 10% (e.g. 33.3, 33.3, 33.3, 0 -> 40, 30, 30 -> 100 after 1 loop, but 10, 10, 10, 10 -> 10, 10, 10, 10 -> final_add = 40, an if loop only added 10 once, resulting in 50, but a while loop is needed to reach 100).
**Learning:** Always use a loop (`while`) when iteratively correcting numerical rounding errors until a target sum is reached, and always guard against division by zero in normalization functions.
