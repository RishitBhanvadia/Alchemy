## YYYY-MM-DD - Duplicated Logic Masking Intent and Conflicting Regimes
**Bug:** There are two different `classifyRegime` functions in the codebase. One in `server/controllers/resultController.js` and another in `server/utils/regimeClassifier.js`. They disagree on the threshold values (`0.60` vs `0.65`) and what happens when other chemicals are present. Also `server/utils/regimeClassifier.js` classifies a solution with 1% acid and 99% catalyst as `ACID_DOMINANT` because `acidBaseSum > 0` and `1 / 1 = 1 > 0.65`.
**Root Cause:** The logic is duplicated, and the version in `regimeClassifier.js` evaluates the ratio `chem_a / (chem_a + chem_b)` without ensuring that A and B are actually the primary components. This causes a single drop of acid in a pure catalyst solution to wrongly flag the entire solution as `ACID_DOMINANT` instead of `CATALYST_DOMINANT`! Additionally, the version in the controller shadows the imported/intended logic but lacks support for `CATALYST_DOMINANT` and `INDICATOR_DOMINANT`.
**Learning:** Always check for duplicate logic across utils/controllers. Ratios should be calculated relative to the *total volume* if we are trying to determine dominance. But since it's `chem_a / (chem_a + chem_b)`, we must first establish that A+B are actually present in significant amounts (e.g., `> 20`) before declaring the solution acid or base dominant.
