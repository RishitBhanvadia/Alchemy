## 2026-03-20 - Discovery of Alchemistry Core App and Key Competitors
**Market Insight:** The market for virtual chemistry laboratories is targeted towards high schools and universities seeking safe, remote, and cost-effective ways to deliver practical chemistry education. Competitors like Labster, PraxiLabs, and ChemCollective focus heavily on interactive, 3D simulations that replicate real-world labs, offering gamified experiences, AI assistance, and curriculum-aligned experiments. Labster emphasizes immersive STEM learning experiences, while ChemCollective focuses on linking chemical computations with authentic lab tasks.

**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry operates precisely in this space. The app provides a "3D PHYSICS LABORATORY" where users (students) can mix chemicals (HCl, NaOH, BTB, MnO2) using interactive sliders to simulate pouring. It includes modules for Titration, Organic Chemistry, and Inorganic Chemistry. Key existing features include a teacher dashboard for analytics, student experiment history, and a basic AI tutor.

**Opportunity:** There is a significant opportunity to enhance Alchemistry's educational value by taking cues from competitors. Labster and PraxiLabs heavily utilize curriculum-aligned quizzes and deeper AI assistance (e.g., PraxiLabs' "Oxi"). Alchemistry can expand its AI tutor to be more integrated during experiments, add gamified elements (like quizzes or achievements after successful reactions), and implement more comprehensive progress tracking and analytics that competitors offer to stand out.
