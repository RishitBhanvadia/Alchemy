## 2026-05-05 - Gamification and Export Gaps in Virtual EdTech

**Codebase Match:** The Alchemistry app has a basic history/result tracking system (`client/src/pages/history.jsx`) and classroom management but lacks gamified milestones for students and exportable data for teachers.
**Opportunity:** Implement a gamification badge system and a CSV export feature for experiment results to align with market expectations and improve user retention.
