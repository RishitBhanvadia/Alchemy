## 2024-05-30 - Virtual Chemistry Lab Market Research
**Market Insight:** The virtual lab market is shifting away from purely open-ended sandboxes (like older flash games) toward structured, educator-driven platforms (like Labster and PraxiLabs) that emphasize assessment, LMS integration, and built-in scaffolding (e.g., auto-grading unknown concentrations).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently excels at open-ended 3D visualization (e.g., `client/src/pages/lab.jsx`) but lacks built-in assessments or structured guidance. Its titration module (`titration.jsx`) simply tracks volume rather than testing the student's ability to calculate unknowns, and its history logs (`history.jsx`) cannot be exported for grading.
**Opportunity:** Bridge the gap between exploration and instruction by implementing lightweight assessment features: add an "Unknown Concentration" mode to the Titration module, integrate real-time pH graphing, and add CSV export functionality to the History page for easy grading.
