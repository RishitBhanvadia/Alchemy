## 2024-05-14 - First Run
**Market Insight:** Virtual chemistry labs (like Labster and PraxiLabs) heavily feature guided learning, safety simulations (like escaping a lab based on chemical properties), custom quizzes, and AI tutors for real-time guidance.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently has a 3D Lab, AI Tutor integration (`AiTutorPanel`), and basic assignments. It lacks comprehensive guided interactive tutorials or safety-focused interactive scenarios.
**Opportunity:** Implementing step-by-step interactive guidance or a safety checklist before experiments would align Alchemistry with top market expectations.
