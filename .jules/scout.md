## 2024-03-14 - Alchemistry Market Positioning
**Market Insight:** The virtual chemistry lab software market (VR/Web) heavily relies on gamification (Futuclass), robust simulation fidelity (Vic's Science Studio, PraxiLabs), and integration with teacher workflows/assignments (Gizmos).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently has a solid 3D simulation foundation and basic teacher/student tracking but lacks strong onboarding, structured objectives, and easy export functionality for students.
**Opportunity:** By implementing guided onboarding, real-time feedback mechanisms, and report exporting, Alchemistry can elevate its educational value and compete more effectively with established platforms in the EdTech space.
