## 2024-05-18 - The Importance of Lab Safety & Guided vs. Open Environments in Virtual Labs
**Market Insight:** Top virtual chemistry simulators (Labster, PraxiLabs) strictly enforce virtual Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) checks (e.g., goggles, gloves) before allowing students to start experiments. They also differentiate between "Guided" (step-by-step instructions with highlighted UI) and "Open" (freeform) modes.
**Codebase Match:** Our app currently allows users to immediately start clicking buttons and mixing chemicals (e.g., in `titration.jsx` and `Lab3D.jsx`) without any safety checks or guided onboarding paths. The user relies on physical lab manuals (hardcoded as text) rather than in-app contextual help.
**Opportunity:** Implementing a mandatory, gamified safety checklist (PPE modal) before routing to experiments, and adding a contextual "Lab Manual" drawer or "AI Tutor" to guide users, will significantly elevate the educational value and institutional appeal of the platform with minimal effort.
