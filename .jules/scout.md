## 2024-04-04 - Virtual Lab Market Insights
**Market Insight:** Top competitors in the virtual chemistry space (like Labster and ChemCollective) heavily emphasize bridging the gap between practical simulation and classroom workflows. Two critical features drive adoption: structured onboarding to reduce cognitive load in 3D spaces, and exportable outcomes for assignment submission. Additionally, emphasizing laboratory safety through contextual warnings adds significant educational value.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently provides a robust 3D sandbox (`Lab3D.jsx`) and tracks experiment results (`history.jsx`, `useHistoryStore`), but lacks a guided tour for first-time users and a mechanism to export these results for teacher review. The existing state management (`useLabStore`) and React architecture make adding these features highly feasible.
**Opportunity:** By implementing a guided tutorial overlay in `Lab3D.jsx` and adding a simple PDF/CSV export button to `history.jsx`, Alchemistry can significantly improve its utility for both students (reduced friction) and teachers (easier grading), aligning it closer to established market leaders.
