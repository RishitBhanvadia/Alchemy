## 2026-05-24 - Virtual Chemistry Lab Assessment & Tracking Gap
**Market Insight:** Top virtual chemistry platforms (Labster, PraxiLabs) differentiate themselves not just through realistic 3D simulations, but through robust instructor analytics, exportable data, and integrated assessments (quizzes/questions tied to experiment results).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry has a solid 3D simulation foundation and an AI tutor but lacks built-in assessment tools, experiment data export features, and detailed instructor analytics on student performance.
**Opportunity:** Adding an "Export to CSV" button on the history page and expanding the Teacher Dashboard with detailed student success analytics are high-priority, relatively easy-to-implement features that align with market expectations.
