## 2024-05-09 - Scout Initialisation
**Market Insight:** The virtual chemistry lab market (Labster, PraxiLabs, Beyond Labz) is heavily driven by gamification, instant feedback, real-world constraints (safety, costs), and guided experiments, but missing key differentiators can cause user drop-off.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry uses React, Three.js, and Supabase to provide a 3D sandbox but lacks structured, guided experiment walkthroughs common in top-tier products.
**Opportunity:** Creating actionable feature proposals tailored to Alchemistry's existing architecture (e.g., building on `Lab3D.jsx` and `history.jsx`).
