## 2024-05-10 - EdTech Virtual Labs Insights
**Market Insight:** High-end virtual labs like Labster and PraxiLabs emphasize seamless LMS integration, gamification (achievements/points), built-in quiz builders for teachers, and detailed performance tracking for individual students.
**Codebase Match:** The app already has user roles (student/teacher), an AI tutor, history, and a TeacherDashboard with StudentAnalyticsChart. It lacks LMS integration, gamified achievements for students, and quiz/assignment builders.
**Opportunity:** Adding gamified achievements, a built-in quiz builder, or an LMS export feature would close the gap with premium products.
