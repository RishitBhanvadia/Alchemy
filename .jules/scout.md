## 2024-05-21 - Integrated Lab Manual
**Market Insight:** Virtual labs often struggle with balancing instruction and simulation. Many rely on external PDFs or textbooks, creating friction.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry explicitly references "Refer Your Chemistry Lab Manual Page - 70" in the UI (`client/src/pages/organic.jsx`), creating a dependency on a physical book that may not exist for the digital user.
**Opportunity:** Integrate the lab manual directly into the application as a collapsible side panel or step-by-step guide. This transforms the tool from a "supplement" to a complete, standalone educational platform, significantly increasing its value proposition.
