## 2025-02-19 - Virtual Lab Export Requirement
**Market Insight:** Top virtual labs like Labster and Beyond Labz consider data export a table-stakes feature so students can submit lab reports to their LMS (Canvas/Blackboard).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry's `history.jsx` and `historyStore` track logs locally, but lack any export functionality.
**Opportunity:** Adding a simple CSV export button to the History page allows this standalone app to integrate better into traditional school workflows without needing full LTI LMS integration.
