## 2024-06-21 - Initial Research
**Market Insight:** Competitors like Labster and PraxiLabs emphasize quizzes and progressive assessment connected directly to virtual lab simulations to ensure comprehension before, during, or after experiments.
**Codebase Match:** The app has a React Three Fiber `Lab3D` component and tracks experiment history via `historyStore`, but lacks an assessment or quiz mechanism.
**Opportunity:** Introduce a post-experiment quiz feature or an in-lab assessment modal connected to the existing experiment flow to validate student learning, increasing the educational value.
