## 2025-05-15 - Discovery of Virtual Science Labs Market Patterns
**Market Insight:** Products like Labster and ChemCollective emphasize "Storytelling" and "Scenario-based learning" to increase engagement, and provide a variety of realistic tools for students to interact with. PhET emphasizes high accessibility with features like alternative input and interactive highlights.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently has standard slider-based inputs and generic reactions but lacks rich narrative scenarios and deep accessibility options.
**Opportunity:** Integrate narrative-driven assignments and enhance accessibility features (keyboard controls, screen reader support) to match industry standards and improve engagement.
