## 2026-06-23 - Bridging the Gap Between Sandbox and Structured Learning
**Market Insight:** The virtual lab simulator market (dominated by Labster, PhET, and ChemCollective) has evolved. While pure sandboxes (like early PhET) are great for exploration, institutions and educators heavily favor platforms with structured learning paths, formative assessments (like embedded quizzes), and robust analytics (like Labster).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently excels at providing an interactive 3D sandbox experience with real-time reaction visualisations (`Lab3D.jsx`, `PhysicsLab.jsx`). However, it lacks the structured guidance and in-process assessment that top competitors use to ensure learning outcomes. The codebase has a solid foundation for state management and basic analytics (e.g., `StudentAnalyticsChart.jsx`, `experiment_results` table), which can be extended.
**Opportunity:** By introducing "Embedded Knowledge Checks" (quizzes that appear during experiments) and "In-Lab Procedural Checklists" (guiding students step-by-step), Alchemistry can transition from a pure sandbox to a structured educational tool. This directly addresses the market's demand for measurable learning outcomes while leveraging its existing strong 3D capabilities. This is highly implementable by extending existing React components (`Lab3D.jsx`, `ResultModal.jsx`) and Supabase schemas.
