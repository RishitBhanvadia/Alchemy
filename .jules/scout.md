## 2026-05-21 - Educational Virtual Lab Market Insights
**Market Insight:** Top virtual chemistry labs like PraxiLabs and ChemVerse provide step-by-step guided procedures, in-experiment quizzes, and comprehensive experiment worksheets (aim, materials, procedure, observations) for students preparing for exams.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently provides a freeform 3D lab environment (`client/src/pages/Lab3D.jsx`) but lacks guided steps, observation logging, or in-experiment knowledge checks to validate learning during an experiment.
**Opportunity:** Introduce an "Experiment Logbook" and "Guided Mode" panel into the `Lab3D` component to bridge the gap between simulation and structured educational outcomes.
