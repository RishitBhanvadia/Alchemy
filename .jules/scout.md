2024-05-18 - EdTech Virtual Labs

**Market Insight:** Top virtual lab simulators (Labster, PraxiLabs) all emphasize real-world contextualization, guided learning pathways, and data export functionalities (like CSV download for lab results) to support robust scientific method practices.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry has a solid 3D simulation foundation, but lacks contextual real-world scenarios and the ability for students or teachers to export their experiment history.
**Opportunity:** Introduce a CSV export feature in the History page and integrate guided, real-world case studies into the experiment modules.
