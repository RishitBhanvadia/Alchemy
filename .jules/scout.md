## 2026-04-22 - Integrated Lab Manuals & Quizzes are Table Stakes
**Market Insight:** All major competitors in the virtual chemistry space (Labster, PraxiLabs, ChemVerse) have moved away from requiring physical lab manuals and instead integrate step-by-step guidance, digital manuals, and quizzes directly into the UI.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently relies on external plain-text references ("Refer Your Chemistry Lab Manual Page - 70") and lacks a formalized assessment mechanism (quizzes) to validate learning outcomes post-experiment.
**Opportunity:** Implementing an in-app toggleable "Lab Manual" and a post-experiment "Quiz Modal" will significantly align the application with modern educational standards and make it a complete, self-contained learning tool.
