## 2026-04-23 - Virtual Lab Interactivity Gaps
**Market Insight:** Top virtual chemistry labs (like PhET, Vic's Science Studio, ChemCollective) lean heavily on gamification and real-time interaction (e.g., drag-and-drop glassware, pouring mechanisms).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry uses sliders and buttons to control chemical amounts, which is functional but lacks the tactile "hands-on" feel of pouring. The 3D scene allows rotation but interaction is limited.
**Opportunity:** Implement a drag-and-drop mechanism for adding chemicals or a pouring animation, elevating the realism.
