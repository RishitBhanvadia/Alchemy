## 2024-05-27 - Virtual Chemistry Lab Market Research
**Market Insight:** The virtual chemistry lab market (competitors like Labster, ChemCollective) focuses heavily on bridging open-ended simulation with structured pedagogy (guided experiments) and practical utility (data export for lab reports).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry has a strong 3D simulation and AI tutor foundation but lacks the standard "export results" feature and structured onboarding found in top competitors.
**Opportunity:** The highest ROI, lowest effort improvement is adding a CSV export feature to the `history.jsx` page, allowing students to download their experiment logs for real-world lab reports.
