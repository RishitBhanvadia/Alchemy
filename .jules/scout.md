## 2024-05-24 - Scout Market Gaps
**Market Insight:** Major market gaps for Alchemistry include a lack of safety protocols (virtual PPE checks), guided onboarding tours, and real-time data analysis (e.g., titration graphs), which are standard features in competitors like PraxiLabs and Labster.
**Codebase Match:** The app has individual modules (titration, organic, inorganic) but lacks contextual guided learning, real-time theoretical explanations (like titration graphs), or gamified elements (PPE checks before entering lab).
**Opportunity:** Introduce a Virtual PPE verification step before entering the Lab, Guided onboarding tooltips, and real-time visualization of data (like titration curves) to match market expectations and improve educational value.
