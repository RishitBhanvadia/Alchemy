## 2024-06-05 - Scout Market Research Report

**Market Insight:** Virtual chemistry labs (like PraxiLabs and Labster) differentiate heavily on scenario-based learning and safety training, rather than just unstructured chemical mixing.

**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently provides a 3D mixing environment (Lab3D) with AI hints and student dashboard. However, it lacks scenario-based tasks (like an "Escape Room" or guided experiment protocols) and specific pre-lab safety modules.

**Opportunity:** Introduce guided experiment protocols (scenarios) and pre-lab safety checklists. This directly aligns with the "table-stakes" of virtual labs and can be implemented by adding new components to the existing React application, utilizing the existing Supabase backend for tracking completion.
