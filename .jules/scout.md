## 2026-05-08 - Market Research: Labster & PhET Insights
**Market Insight:** Leading virtual labs differentiate through structured gamification (Labster's "Escape the Lab") and deep accessibility features like interactive voicing and alternative keyboard inputs (PhET).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry has a solid 3D sandbox and basic screen-reader text, but lacks structured narrative challenges and active voicing/alternative keyboard controls.
**Opportunity:** Implementing alternative keyboard controls (WASD) for sliders and introducing a simple gamified mode or specific practice activities (like balancing equations) would significantly close the gap with top competitors.
