## 2025-02-18 - Alchemistry Market Research
**Market Insight:** Top virtual chemistry labs (Labster, Beyond Labz) prioritize robust data export and in-context conceptual learning, ensuring students can easily submit reports and understand chemical properties *before* interaction.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry has a great 3D foundation and tracks history (`useHistoryStore`), but lacks CSV export for that history and tooltips on chemical controls in `Lab3D.jsx`.
**Opportunity:** Implementing CSV export for history logs and adding educational tooltips to the sliders are low-effort, high-impact features that bridge the gap with market leaders.
