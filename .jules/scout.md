## 2023-10-24 - Virtual Lab Data Export
**Market Insight:** Top virtual chemistry labs (Labster, PraxiLabs) universally allow students and teachers to export experimental data for lab reports and grading.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry tracks experiments via `historyStore.js` and displays them in `history.jsx`, but lacks an export function.
**Opportunity:** Adding a CSV Export button to `history.jsx` is a high-value, low-effort feature that directly bridges a major feature gap.
