# Scout's Journal

## 2026-02-23 - Bridging the Gap Between Simulation and Education
**Market Insight:** The virtual lab market is bifurcated into high-end, immersive simulations (Labster) and accessible, interactive tools (PhET). A critical missing piece in many mid-tier tools is the bridge to *formal assessment*.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry has a strong 3D visualization engine but currently acts as a "sandbox". It lacks the data portability (exporting results) and structured guidance (tutorials) required for it to be a viable *assignment* tool for teachers.
**Opportunity:** By adding simple "Lab Report" export functionality (CSV/PDF) and a basic onboarding flow, Alchemistry can immediately compete as a lightweight, accessible alternative to expensive platforms like Labster for schools that need verifyable student work.
