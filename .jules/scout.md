## 2024-06-10 - Virtual Chemistry Lab Market Research
**Market Insight:** The virtual chemistry lab market (competitors like Labster, PraxiLabs, ChemCollective) is divided between sandbox environments and structured curriculum tools. The most successful platforms bridge this gap by offering guided scenarios and robust instructor analytics, alongside table-stakes features like CSV data export.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently has a rich 3D sandbox (`Lab3D.jsx`) and foundational classroom/assignment data structures (`assignmentStore.js`, `TeacherDashboard.jsx`). However, it lacks in-lab historical context (the UI for history exists in CSS but not JSX) and basic data export capabilities.
**Opportunity:** By implementing quick-win features like CSV export for history/dashboards and a slide-out history panel in the 3D lab (using already written CSS), Alchemistry can significantly improve its utility for both students and instructors, moving closer to mid-tier competitors.
