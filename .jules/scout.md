## 2024-05-18 - Teacher-Centric Data Flow & Guided Virtual Labs
**Market Insight:** Top virtual lab competitors (e.g., Labster, Futuclass) differentiate heavily through seamless LMS integrations, exportable progress metrics for teachers, and guided/gamified student onboarding rather than just sandbox environments.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently provides a rich 3D sandbox (`Lab3D.jsx`) and a rudimentary teacher dashboard (`TeacherDashboard.jsx` with an analytics data grid), but lacks structured onboarding/tooltips and an export mechanism for student data.
**Opportunity:** Implementing CSV export in the Teacher Dashboard and a contextual guided onboarding overlay in the 3D Lab are low-effort, high-impact features that bridge the gap to enterprise-grade educational tools.
