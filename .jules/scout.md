## 2024-05-18 - Market Research Context

**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry has a great sandbox lab (Lab3D.jsx) but assignment tracking is disconnected from the lab experience (assignmentStore.js).
**Opportunity:** Connecting the assignment system directly into the Lab3D environment to provide guided instruction and track completions.
