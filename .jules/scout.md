## 2024-05-18 - Alchemistry Virtual Labs Competitive Landscape
**Market Insight:** The virtual chemistry laboratory market is dominated by established players like Labster and PhET Interactive Simulations. A key differentiator in top platforms is the integration of mandatory safety training (PPE) and structured, guided onboarding before users interact with chemicals, simulating real-world lab protocols. Additionally, teachers expect robust analytics with data export capabilities (e.g., CSV) to integrate with existing grading systems.
**Codebase Match:** The `client/src/pages/Lab3D.jsx` component currently provides an open sandbox environment but lacks a structured safety or onboarding flow. The `client/src/pages/TeacherDashboard.jsx` provides basic score distribution charts (`StudentAnalyticsChart.jsx`) but no way to export this data.
**Opportunity:** Implementing a pre-experiment safety check (e.g., clicking on virtual goggles/gloves) and a guided tooltip onboarding in `Lab3D.jsx` will align the app with market expectations. Adding a CSV export button to `TeacherDashboard.jsx` is a high-impact, low-effort feature that fulfills a common teacher requirement in this space.
