## 2026-04-19 - Bridging Sandbox and Structured Assessment
**Market Insight:** Top virtual labs (Labster, Beyond Labz) prioritize structured, quantitative assessment over pure visual simulation, focusing on realistic constraints (temperature, measurement) and exportable data for educators.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry's UI is highly polished and accessible, but currently functions mostly as a qualitative sandbox (adjusting generic concentration sliders).
**Opportunity:** We can rapidly close the gap with premium tools by implementing lightweight quantitative features—specifically, interactive digital readouts (pH/Temp), exportable CSV reports from the History page, and injecting real-time lab state into the AI Tutor for contextual guidance.
