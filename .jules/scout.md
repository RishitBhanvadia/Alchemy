## 2025-05-15 - Alchemistry Market Research
**Market Insight:** Virtual chemistry labs emphasize a mix of highly realistic 3D environments, gamification, and robust analytics for educators. Key competitors include Labster, PraxiLabs, and Beyond Labz. Table-stakes features include diverse experiment types, safety-focused virtual manipulation, and performance analytics. Differentiators include AI assistants, VR integration, and custom quiz builders.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry already has a solid 3D base (React Three Fiber), basic experiments (organic, inorganic, titration), and role-based access (student/teacher dashboards). It lacks deeper gamification, custom quiz building, advanced analytics (like a teacher being able to see exactly where a student got stuck), and an AI lab assistant that provides real-time guidance based on the current state of the experiment.
**Opportunity:**
1. Enhance the existing AI Tutor to be context-aware of the current experiment state.
2. Add a custom quiz/assignment builder for teachers.
3. Introduce gamification elements like XP, badges, or a streak system.
