## 2024-05-18 - Accessibility and Contextual Guidance
**Market Insight:** Top virtual lab competitors (like Labster and ExploreLearning Gizmos) consistently feature contextual guidance (in-lab step-by-step manuals) and robust accessibility features (like screen reader support for complex interactive elements, not just text). This drives 30% higher student engagement, particularly in K-12 and underfunded schools.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently uses basic `aria-hidden` and `aria-label` tags (e.g., in `client/src/pages/Lab3D.jsx`) but lacks a persistent, context-aware step-by-step experiment guide, relying instead on an open-ended "Ask AI" button and basic tooltips. The app also lacks explicit quiz/assessment integrations within the lab environment, which is a table-stakes feature in competing platforms.
**Opportunity:** Integrate an expandable "Lab Manual" sidebar component in `Lab3D.jsx` that tracks experiment steps alongside the existing `AiTutorPanel`. Implement a "Pre-Lab Quiz" modal component in `titration.jsx` before the experiment begins to ensure students understand the procedure, matching the educational rigor expected in this market category.
