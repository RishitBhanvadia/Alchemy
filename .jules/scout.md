## 2025-02-14 - Initial Learning
**Market Insight:** Virtual Lab Simulators are heavily used in STEM education, providing safe, scalable, and interactive 3D simulations of lab experiments (organic, inorganic, titrations).
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry is exactly this - a 3D virtual chemistry lab built with React/Three.js, aiming to serve as a digital alternative for schools.
**Opportunity:** Expanding the core feature set, enhancing realism with detailed tools, integrating better assessment/quiz loops, providing real-time hints, offering data export, and increasing gamification are immediate opportunities to compete with top platforms like Labster and PraxiLabs.
