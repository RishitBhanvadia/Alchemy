## 2024-05-24 - The Need for Guided Experiments in Virtual Sandbox Environments
**Market Insight:** Leading virtual lab competitors (like Labster and PraxiLabs) do not just offer open-ended 3D sandboxes; they heavily integrate step-by-step guidance, storytelling, and post-experiment quizzes.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently provides a strong, free-form 3D sandbox in `Lab3D.jsx` but lacks in-ui guidance. Users might feel lost without an integrated lab manual to bridge the gap between pure exploration and learning objectives.
**Opportunity:** Integrate an "Interactive Step-by-Step Lab Guide" side panel directly into `Lab3D.jsx` that tracks experiment progress, offering a structured learning path alongside the open-ended physics simulation.
