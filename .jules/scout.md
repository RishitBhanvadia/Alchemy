## 2025-10-26 - Safety First in Virtual Labs
**Market Insight:** Top competitors like PraxiLabs and Labster explicitly include safety protocols (like checking for PPE) before allowing students to enter the virtual lab. This reinforces real-world safety habits even in a simulation.
**Codebase Match:** Alchemistry currently allows immediate access to experiments without any safety checks or reminders.
**Opportunity:** Implementing a simple "PPE Check" modal or toggle before entering `Lab.jsx` would be a significant pedagogical differentiator and align with educational standards.
