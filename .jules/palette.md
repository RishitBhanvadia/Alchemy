## 2024-02-23 - Icon-only buttons need aria-labels and titles
**Learning:** In the Alchemistry codebase, icon-only interactive elements require both `aria-label` for screen reader accessibility and a `title` attribute to provide native browser tooltips on hover.
**Action:** Always add both `aria-label` and `title` to icon-only buttons to ensure they are accessible and provide helpful tooltips to all users.
