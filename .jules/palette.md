## 2024-05-06 - Palette: Added ARIA labels to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** React components (like the sidebar navigation, Lab toggle actions, and AI Tutor toggle buttons) in this app often omit `aria-label` attributes on icon-only buttons (like those enclosing FontAwesome or emojis).
**Action:** When creating new interactive elements lacking text context, ensure they include an explicit `aria-label` attribute to improve screen reader accessibility.
