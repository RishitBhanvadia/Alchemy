# Market Research Report
**App:** A web-based virtual chemistry laboratory using React and Three.js for interactive, safe 3D chemistry experiments.
**Market:** EdTech / Virtual STEM Simulations (Higher Ed & High School)
**Date:** 2026-04-29
**Competitors Researched:** Labster, Beyond Labz, PraxiLabs

## Executive Summary
The virtual chemistry lab market is dominated by tools that prioritize not just the *visuals* of experiments, but the *pedagogy* behind them. Top competitors emphasize gamified, inquiry-based learning, safe error-making, and robust student assessment. Alchemistry possesses a strong technical foundation (Three.js 3D environment, Supabase auth, AI Tutor) that matches or exceeds table-stakes features. However, it trails behind in structured, interactive student data collection (notebooks) and scenario-based learning compared to giants like Labster. The biggest opportunity is leveraging the existing AI Tutor and state management to build interactive, assessable learning tools.

## Competitor Analysis
- **Labster:** The market leader. Focuses on immersive storylines, gamified learning, and high-quality 3D environments. Differentiates by wrapping experiments in real-world scenarios (e.g., investigating lake pollution).
- **Beyond Labz:** Focuses on ultra-realistic physics and highly flexible open-ended play. Strongest differentiator is their "time-travel" (fast-forwarding) features and integrated, detailed lab books for raw data collection.
- **PraxiLabs:** Focuses on accessibility (anytime, anywhere) and an AI Lab Assistant ("Oxi"). Differentiates with a custom quiz builder linked directly to experiments and strong LMS integration.

## Gap Analysis

### Table Stakes (Expected by users, missing from app)
- **Integrated Student Lab Notebook:** A dedicated place inside the lab view to write down observations, hypothesize, and record raw data before the system gives the answer.
- **Scenario/Storyline Context:** Currently, experiments are open sandboxes. Market standards expect a "Mission" or "Objective" prompt to guide the learning.

### Differentiating Opportunities (Stand-out features)
- **Time Controls (Fast Forward/Rewind):** Allowing students to speed up slow reactions, a feature highly praised in Beyond Labz.
- **AI-Guided Quizzes:** PraxiLabs uses "Oxi" for guidance. We have `AiTutorPanel.jsx`, but it's passive. We can make it proactively ask questions based on the `reactionState`.

### UX Patterns (Design/interaction patterns common in top products)
- **Walkthrough/Interactive Onboarding:** Guided UI highlighting what to do first.
- **Data Export:** Ability to export experiment logs to CSV/PDF for assignments.

## Prioritised Recommendations

### 1. Interactive Lab Notebook — Priority: HIGH | Effort: MEDIUM
**What:** A side panel where students can type observations and link them to their history logs.
**Why:** Competitors (Beyond Labz) rely on lab books for assessment. It moves the app from a "toy" to an "educational tool."
**Where in code:** Create `client/src/components/LabNotebook.jsx` and integrate it into `client/src/pages/Lab3D.jsx` alongside the History Panel.
**How:** Add a new Supabase table or JSON column in `experiment_logs` for "user_notes". Bind a text area to this state.

### 2. Scenario-Based Learning Prompts — Priority: HIGH | Effort: SMALL
**What:** Add a "Mission Brief" overlay before starting an experiment.
**Why:** Labster's main draw is real-world scenarios. This grounds the abstract 3D sandbox.
**Where in code:** Update `client/src/pages/StudentDashboard.jsx` to pass a `mission` prop or query param to the lab routes, and display it in `Lab3D.jsx`.
**How:** Create a simple modal `MissionBriefModal.jsx` that shows a short story (e.g., "Synthesize water") before the `Lab3D` canvas unlocks.

### 3. Proactive AI Tutor Quizzing — Priority: MEDIUM | Effort: MEDIUM
**What:** The AI Tutor should ask a quick multiple-choice question when a reaction finishes, before showing the final result.
**Why:** PraxiLabs uses this to ensure students aren't just clicking blindly.
**Where in code:** `client/src/components/AiTutorPanel.jsx` and `ResultModal.jsx`.
**How:** Intercept the `handlePlayClick` success in `Lab3D.jsx`. Before setting `isResultOpen(true)`, pop up a question generated by the AI based on the `currentHint` or chemicals used.

### 4. Experiment Data Export (CSV) — Priority: MEDIUM | Effort: SMALL
**What:** A button to export the user's experiment history to a CSV file.
**Why:** Table stakes for teachers to grade off-platform or students to use in reports.
**Where in code:** `client/src/pages/history.jsx` or the `Lab3D` History panel.
**How:** Add an `ExportButton` component that maps the `useHistoryStore` logs to a CSV blob and triggers a download.

### 5. Time-Scale Controls — Priority: LOW | Effort: LARGE
**What:** A slider to speed up the 3D physics engine and reaction times.
**Why:** Beyond Labz uses this heavily. Prevents student boredom on slow reactions.
**Where in code:** `client/src/components/3d-animations/PhysicsLab.jsx` and `Lab3D.jsx`.
**How:** Pass a `timeScale` prop to the Three.js clock/animations.

### 6. Walkthrough / Onboarding Tour — Priority: LOW | Effort: MEDIUM
**What:** A first-time user tour highlighting the acid/base sliders and AI tutor.
**Why:** Ensures students know how to use the open sandbox.
**Where in code:** `client/src/pages/Lab3D.jsx`.
**How:** Implement `react-joyride` or a simple custom overlay sequence controlled by a `hasSeenTour` flag in `localStorage`.

### 7. Interactive Safety Gear Requirement — Priority: LOW | Effort: SMALL
**What:** Force students to "equip" goggles/gloves before the sliders unlock.
**Why:** Labster enforces safety protocols. Builds good lab habits.
**Where in code:** `client/src/pages/Lab3D.jsx`.
**How:** Add a toggle button for "Equip PPE". Disable the "INITIATE REACTION" button until toggled.

### 8. Collaborative / Multiplayer View — Priority: LOW | Effort: LARGE
**What:** See other students' avatars or cursors in the same lab room.
**Why:** Mimics a real classroom environment, a feature emerging in newer VR/web labs.
**Where in code:** `client/src/pages/Lab3D.jsx` and Supabase Realtime.
**How:** Use Supabase presence to broadcast cursor positions over the Canvas.

### 9. Detailed Performance Analytics for Teachers — Priority: MEDIUM | Effort: MEDIUM
**What:** Aggregate time spent, errors made, and notebook entries for teachers.
**Why:** PraxiLabs strongly markets its "Performance Analytics".
**Where in code:** `client/src/pages/TeacherDashboard.jsx`.
**How:** Query `experiment_results` and `experiment_logs` to build simple charts using Recharts.

### 10. Pause / Resume Experiment State — Priority: LOW | Effort: MEDIUM
**What:** Save the exact slider states and reaction progress to the DB.
**Why:** Allows students to stop midway and return later.
**Where in code:** `client/src/store/labStore.js`.
**How:** Add a `saveState` action that persists `chemA`, `chemB`, etc., to a Supabase table.

## Quick Wins (< 1 day each)
1. **Scenario-Based Learning Prompts:** Add a simple static "Mission" string to the dashboard cards that appears as a toast/modal in the Lab.
2. **Experiment Data Export (CSV):** A ~30 line function in the History panel to download logs.
3. **Interactive Safety Gear Requirement:** A simple toggle switch that gates the Initiate Reaction button.
